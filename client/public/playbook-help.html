Fix Playbooks

Short step-by-step fixes for common support problems. You write them here in APM; support staff see them in the Ticket Researcher program.

What a playbook does

  • When a ticket's title contains a playbook's match words, Ticket Researcher shows a yellow banner under the ticket and the steps in its Playbooks tab.
  • Ticket Researcher's AI research also reads matching playbooks and says which ones it relied on.
  • Only playbooks that are switched on and have steps are shown.

Playbooks match on title words only. Ticket Researcher reminds staff to check the ticket really is that problem before following the steps.

The fields

  • Problem: a short name, e.g. "Printer Setup missing in Cash Receipting".
  • Product line (required): TRIO MSI Spectrum OpenWindow ResourceMate. A TRIO playbook only shows on tickets whose TeamSupport product is a TRIO product. The lines work very differently, so the same problem in two lines needs two playbooks, each with that line's own steps.
  • Match words: words or phrases, separated by commas. A ticket matches when its title contains any of them as whole words (capitals don't matter). Add the different ways customers word the problem, e.g. "printer setup, receipt printer".
  • Products (optional): narrow it further to tickets whose product name contains one of these, e.g. "Cash Receipting".
  • Steps: one step per line, in order. Ticket Researcher numbers them.
  • Switched on: tick it once the steps are checked. It can't be switched on without steps.

Writing steps with AI

  1. Fill in the problem, product line and match words.
  2. Click Draft steps with AI. This saves the playbook first.
  3. The AI reads up to 8 matching closed Support tickets from the last year, only from the playbook's product line, and writes steps from how they were fixed. It takes about 1–2 minutes.
  4. Read the Check notes and the list of tickets it used, then edit the steps.
  5. Tick Switched on and click Save.

The AI only reads TeamSupport. Nothing is ever written back to a ticket. Always check its steps before switching a playbook on.

Finding your way around

  • Use Show above the list to see one product line at a time. A new playbook added while a line is shown starts on that line.
  • The Status column says whether each playbook is on, has steps waiting to be checked, or has no steps yet.
  • Delete is inside the edit form. Switching a playbook off is usually better, as it keeps the steps.
  • Settings (left menu) is where you change your password, language and colours.

Getting access

  • An APM admin gives playbook access with the Playbooks checkbox on the Users page.
  • Support accounts without that access can't sign in to APM. They use the Ticket Researcher program only.
